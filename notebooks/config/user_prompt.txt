Extract the exam metadata and all exercises from the attached exam PDF.

The following fields are already known — copy them exactly into your output, do not read them from the PDF:
- exam_id:     {exam_id}
- subject:     {subject}
- source_file: {source_file}

For all other metadata fields (exam_type, session, trimester, filiere, year,
duration_minutes, total_marks, language, region, institution, academic_year)
read them directly from the exam paper and follow the controlled vocabularies
in the system prompt exactly.
